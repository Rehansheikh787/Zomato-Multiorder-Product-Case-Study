# Step 2 — Product Execution & Delivery Planning

This section translates the approved Zomato Multiorder product concept into an execution-ready delivery structure.

> **Portfolio note:** This is a product management case study and concept exploration. It does not represent internal Zomato work or confidential company information.

## Scope

Step 2 intentionally focuses on the two capabilities selected for execution planning:

- **P0 — Multi-Restaurant Cart:** items from multiple eligible nearby restaurants in one cart.
- **P1 — Single Checkout:** one review and payment experience for the selected restaurant orders.

**P2 — Coordinated Delivery** remains part of the broader product concept from Step 1, but it is not included in this execution backlog.
