# Sprint Planning

## Objective

Turn the approved P0 and P1 user stories into an execution-ready backlog while preserving the PM-provided sizing, risk and impact inputs.

## Capability sequence

1. **P0 — Multi-Restaurant Cart**
   - US1 Add Items from Another Restaurant
   - US2 Review Items by Restaurant
   - US3 Modify Items Without Losing Other Selections
   - US4 View Multi-Restaurant Eligibility
2. **P1 — Single Checkout**
   - US5 Review Multi-Restaurant Order
   - US6 View Final Payable Amount
   - US7 Pay for All Orders Together
   - US8 Recover from Payment Failure

## Planning constraints

- S = 1 week, M = 2 weeks, L = 1 month, XL = quarter.
- US3, US7 and US8 carry the highest risk score (5/5).
- US7 is both highest impact (5/5) and high risk (5/5).
- US1 and US5 have finalized P/Q/R/S/T/U acceptance criteria.
- US2, US3, US4, US6, US7 and US8 still require approved acceptance criteria before implementation is considered fully ready.
- The response-time target remains undefined.
- No Total prioritization formula has been approved.

## Week-on-week planning status

The source working session requested week-on-week sprint deliverables, but it did not finalize a specific weekly sequence for all eight stories. A detailed calendar is therefore not presented as an approved plan here.

The delivery backlog and sizing are execution-ready inputs; detailed sprint allocation should be baselined after the remaining acceptance criteria and open technical decisions are resolved.
