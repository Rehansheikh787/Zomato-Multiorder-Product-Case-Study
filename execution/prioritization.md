## Size definitions

- **S** = 1 week
- **M** = 2 weeks
- **L** = 1 month
- **XL** = quarter

Risk and Impact use a **1–5 scale**, where 1 is low and 5 is high.

## Important constraint

The original table includes a **Total** column, but no calculation formula was approved. Therefore, no Total score is calculated in this portfolio.
